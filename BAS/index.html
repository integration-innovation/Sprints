Basic Accessibility Screening

Does Basic Accessibility Legislation apply to this project? Four gates decide it. All four must be met. An IFC model can be read for supporting evidence.

Preliminary screening aid — not a compliance certificate. This restates the applicability triggers in plain form to support early-stage design decisions. It does not replace the prevailing BCA Code on Accessibility in the Built Environment, the plan-approval process, or the judgement of the Qualified Person submitting the works. Nothing read from a model is a compliance finding.

Gate 1 — Nature of the works

Basic Accessibility Legislation is an Additions & Alterations trigger. A new build follows a different and fuller route.

Gate 2 — Existing accessibility status
Is the building already barrier-free?

The legislation targets buildings that are not already barrier-free.

Gate 3 — Predominant use
How is the building used?

Residential and industrial developments sit outside this trigger.

Gate 4 — Scale of the works

The threshold is more than 500 m². Enter the GFA of the works being submitted, not of the whole building, and confirm the measurement basis against the prevailing BCA definition.
