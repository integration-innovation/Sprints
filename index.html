Basic Accessibility Assessment Tool

Singapore · Building Control Act 1989 · BCA Basic Accessibility Legislation · Code on Accessibility in the Built Environment

Preliminary screening aid — not a compliance certificate. This tool reproduces the applicability triggers in plain form to support early-stage design decisions. It does not replace the prevailing BCA Code, the plan-approval process, or the judgement of the Qualified Person submitting the works. Confirm every outcome against the current legislation before relying on it.

Step 1 — Applicability screening
1. Nature of the proposed works

Basic Accessibility Legislation is an Additions & Alterations trigger. New builds follow a different, fuller route.

2. Existing accessibility status of the building

The legislation targets buildings that are not already barrier-free.

3. Predominant use of the building

Residential and industrial developments sit outside this trigger.

The threshold is more than 500 m². Enter the GFA of the works being submitted, not the whole building, and confirm the measurement basis against the prevailing BCA definition.
